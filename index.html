How to read this
  • From the sheet: prices, taken units, last queue number, units taken and drop-outs per day. Source: berlayar_rise_chart_Public.
  • Projection at your turn: a model replays the bookings so far to learn how strongly each stack is favoured and how fast its floors go from the top down. It then plays out the queue ahead of you about 1,250 times with random drop-outs and the Chinese quota of each block. The result is averaged over five assumptions about how loyal buyers stay to a favoured stack once only lower floors remain. One earlier snapshot barely tells them apart, which is why the ranges are wide.
  • Appointment date: uses community reports and estimates beyond the latest shared date. Bookings are separately recorded through 20 November.
